Our story

A small shop built from the dedication of two people

who wanted to create a new experience for our customers.

Our passion began as customers ourselves — seeking the best in both taste and service. That desire became the starting point of After Layers.

Every product we create is thoughtfully developed under one simple belief: what we offer our customers must always be of quality. Thank you for choosing After Layers. We truly hope to be part of your moments.
